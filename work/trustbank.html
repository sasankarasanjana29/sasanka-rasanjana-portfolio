Overview

Why TrustBank

Banking apps lose people at the same moments: logging in, sending money and finding proof that a payment went through. I wanted those moments to feel fast, clear and trustworthy.

The goal

Cut the effort in everyday tasks like checking balances, transferring money and finding history, in a way that works for every generation and comfort level in Sri Lanka.

My role

I designed the app end to end: flows, wireframes, the visual system and a clickable prototype, based on my thesis research.

Research

Built on my thesis about how different generations in Sri Lanka use mobile apps: survey insights, expert input and a pilot test. I also took apart the banking apps people already use.

Apps people use today

  • Dense home screens, small tap targets and features buried in menus.
  • Transfers with no clear review step, and receipts that are hard to find.
01

Keep it calm

People want a minimal interface so they can scan quickly and avoid mistakes when money is involved.

02

Safe, not exhausting

03

Proof, right away

The problem

Different generations

One design has to work for a 20 year old and a 60 year old alike.

Why it mattered

Banking is high stakes. When people feel unsure at login or payment they slow down, make mistakes or stop trusting the app. Smoother flows build confidence and move more people to digital banking.

Final screens

Sign in and verification

  • Language comes first, so everything after it reads clearly.
  • One-time codes show clear error and locked states, so nobody gets stuck.
  • A passcode plus Face ID or Touch ID makes coming back fast and still feel secure.

Home and accounts

  • Balances and accounts up front, quick actions for everyday tasks, and recent activity at a glance.

Pay and transfer

  • Saved recipients a tap away; a short, structured form adds new ones without errors.
  • The amount screen shows the fee before you commit.

Review, confirm and proof

  • A review step shows recipient, amount and fee before any money moves.
  • Every payment ends with a receipt to share or save.

Transactions and export

  • A history built for quick scanning, details with every reference, and export for records.

Notifications and security alerts

  • Alerts say what happened and what to do next: approve or report, in one tap.
