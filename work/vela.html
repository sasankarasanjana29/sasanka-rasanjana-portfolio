VELA

A shopping app prototype from my undergraduate research, made so Gen Z, Millennial and Gen X shoppers in Sri Lanka can search, decide and check out faster.

Overview

Why VELA

Mobile shoppers slow down at the same three points: searching, deciding and paying. VELA makes each of those faster and clearer.

The goal

Smooth the whole journey, from discovery to checkout, tracking and returns, with an interface that feels modern, minimal and trustworthy.

My role

I designed the flows, the visual system and the prototype, based on my thesis research.

Research

Mixed methods, across three generations of Sri Lankan shoppers.

~300survey responses from Gen Z, Millennials and Gen X
9expert interviews
60people in a moderated usability pilot, 20 per generation
01

Clean wins

Every generation strongly preferred a minimal, uncluttered interface.

02

Different priorities

Gen X valued a fast checkout most; Millennials cared most about easy search and reviews.

03

So

Discovery and checkout had to be the strongest parts of the app. The pilot shaped labels, step count, tap targets, contrast and trust cues.

Apps people use today

  • Busy home screens, hidden filters and checkout totals that appear late.

What shoppers wrote in reviews

  • Real store reviews: missing prices in search, slow refunds and orders marked delivered that never arrived.

The problem

01

Slow discovery

Search and filters are not obvious, so finding the right product takes too long.

02

Low confidence

Ratings, delivery and return details are hidden on product pages.

03

Checkout friction

Too many steps and totals that are unclear until the end.

04

Dead-end payments

A failed payment gives no clear way to recover.

05

Afterthought tracking

Order tracking and returns are hard to find.

06

Clutter

Busy screens directly cost speed and clarity.

Why it mattered

Shopping is a repeat habit, so small friction adds up. Every extra step or unclear total raises drop-offs and lowers trust.

Final screens

Sign in

  • Quick sign-in and a guest mode let people try the app first.
  • Language comes first, removing a barrier across generations.
  • Password guidance cuts failed sign-ups.

Discover and search

  • Clear categories and scannable product cards.
  • Recent and top searches save typing; filters are light and easy to undo.

Product page

  • Price, rating, variants, key details and the call to action, in that order.

Cart

  • Items can be removed with a swipe and brought back with Undo.
  • The total is always visible.

Pay, and recover when it fails

  • Predictable steps: cart, address, delivery, payment.
  • Success and failure both offer a next step: track the order, retry or change the method.
