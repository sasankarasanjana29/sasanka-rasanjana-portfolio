Pouch

A free, private budgeting app for iPhone, shaped by what 1,751 App Store reviews of a popular budgeting app asked for, with Hedge the hedgehog as a guide.

What the reviews said

I collected 1,751 public App Store reviews of a popular budgeting app, from 12 countries, and grouped every complaint.

24%said bank sync breaks or their bank is not supported
23%were upset about paying, or seeing the price only after setup
54%of one star reviews mention one of those two
01

Crashes and bugs

8% of reviews: the app crashes or will not open.

02

Confusing money

Wallets, balances, cards and transfers are hard to follow.

03

Tedious setup

No tutorial, and setup takes too long.

What Pouch does instead

No bank login, ever

You log a spend in about three taps, and everything stays on your phone.

Free

No subscription and no paywall waiting after setup.

Private

One network call in the whole app: exchange rates, nothing about you.

Any currency, 13 languages

Income paid from abroad can come in 38 currencies, and Pouch speaks 13 languages, including Sinhala and Tamil.

Screens

Hedge, your guide

  • A counted week gets a celebration: Hedge reacts as you log and is proud of you.
  • A new level comes with its own moment, and every entry counts toward it.
  • Gems buy Hedge new looks from a wardrobe of 51 outfits, like hats, glasses and colours.

Setup and today

  • First run asks when you get paid, how much and the money you have now.
  • Today shows what is safe to spend, your savings goal and every pot at a glance.
  • A new entry takes about three taps: type the amount, pick a pot, save.

Budget, pots and family colours

  • Pots sit in families like Housing and Food and drinks, each with what is left.
  • Each pot gets its own colour and a round icon, picked from one grid.
  • The overview shows what came in, what went out and where it went.

Any currency, any language

  • Income paid in another currency is converted at today's rate, with the rate source shown.
  • 38 currencies to pick from, like US dollar, Euro and British pound.
  • Pouch speaks 13 languages, picked in Profile, including Sinhala and Tamil.
  • Every screen switches at once: here Today in Sinhala, with your own pot names kept as you typed them.

Entries, wallets and big buys

  • Entries open on a totals strip, then what is coming up and each day in its own group.
  • Wallets keep accounts, savings and cards apart, with your net worth on top.
  • Big buys are paid off in parts, next to card plans and what you own.

Missions, insights and your data

  • Profile shows Hedge's level, weeks tracked and weekly missions that pay out gems.
  • Insights encourage instead of scold, with notes from Hedge and the last six months.
  • Your data stays on the phone: back up, restore, and export or import entries as CSV.
  • Deleting everything takes a deliberate hold, never a single tap.

Dark mode

  • Today in dark mode, with every colour checked for contrast.
  • A new entry in dark mode.
  • Budget in dark mode, with the pot colours kept readable.
