Pouch

A free, private budgeting app for iPhone, built to fix what people hate about Buddy, with Hedge the hedgehog as a guide.

What the reviews said

I read 1,751 App Store reviews of Buddy, a popular budgeting app, from 12 countries, and grouped every complaint.

24%said bank sync breaks or their bank is not supported
23%were upset about paying, or seeing the price only after setup
47%of one star reviews come from just those two
01

Crashes and bugs

8% of reviews: the app crashes or will not open.

02

Confusing money

Wallets, balances, cards and transfers are hard to follow.

03

Tedious setup

No tutorial, and setup takes too long.

What Pouch does instead

No bank login, ever

You log a spend in about three taps, and everything stays on your phone.

Free

No subscription and no paywall waiting after setup.

Private

One network call in the whole app: the US dollar rate, nothing about you.

Screens

Setup and today

  • First run asks for payday, pay and the money you have now, then shows what is safe to spend today.

Budget, pots and wallets

  • Pots give every rupee a job; wallets keep accounts and cards apart.

Plans and insights

  • Big buys spread over months, a savings goal, and charts that encourage instead of scold.

Hedge and the game layer

  • Celebrations for a counted week and a new level, and a story that unlocks as you go.

Dark mode and widgets

  • Every colour checked for contrast in both modes, and Home Screen widgets with your own numbers.
