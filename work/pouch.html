What the reviews said

I collected 1,751 public App Store reviews of a popular budgeting app, from 12 countries, and grouped every complaint.

How I made this

I directed the product and designed the experience, shaping the screens directly in the working app on the simulator and my own iPhone. The code was written with Claude Code, an AI coding tool, working from my direction. The research is desk research: public App Store reviews, read and grouped by theme, not interviews or user testing. The website is live, and the app is not on the App Store yet.

What Pouch does instead

No bank login, ever

You log a spend in about three taps, or paste your bank's text and Pouch fills it in. Everything stays on your phone.

Free

No subscription and no paywall waiting after setup.

Private

One network call in the whole app: exchange rates, nothing about you.

Any currency, 13 languages

Income paid from abroad can come in 38 currencies, and Pouch speaks 13 languages, including Sinhala and Tamil.

What I cut and why

Two things I built, then took out, and what replaced them.

Red when you overspend

  • Idea A spending meter that turned red past your plan.
  • Cut Guilt makes people stop logging, and logging is the whole habit.
  • Now It says Rs X extra in grey, and Hedge gives every entry a thumbs up.

A minus balance

  • Idea Safe to spend going below zero.
  • Cut A minus number reads as failure on the screen you open most.
  • Now Safe today stops at 0.

Screens

Hedge, your guide

  • Hedge cheers every week you track.
  • Every entry counts toward the next level.
  • Gems buy him outfits from a wardrobe of 51.

Weeks tracked and Hedge's trip

  • Weeks, not days, so a quiet day at home never breaks it.
  • Spent nothing? Mark the day and it still counts.
  • Tap Hedge for his trip: every tracked day is a step, and each week he meets a neighbour.

Setup and today

  • Hedge says hi, then asks five quick questions.
  • Split your pay counts down to All planned.
  • Today shows what is safe to spend.

Budget, categories and family colours

  • Categories sit in families, each with what is left.
  • Every category has its own colour and icon.
  • The overview shows where the month went.

Any currency, any language

  • Income has its own categories, like Salary.
  • Paid in another currency? Pouch converts at today's rate.
  • 38 currencies, with yours on top.
  • 13 languages, including Sinhala and Tamil.

Entries, wallets and big buys

  • Every entry by day, with what is coming up.
  • Every wallet in one list, with net worth on top.
  • Big buys paid off in parts.

Missions, insights and your data

  • Levels, weeks tracked and weekly missions.
  • Insights that encourage, never scold.
  • Your data stays on your phone.

Add after you pay

  • Three ways, easiest first: Paste, Siri, Shortcuts.
  • Copy a bank text and Paste bank text appears.
  • One tap fills in the amount and shop.
  • Or tell Siri, and Pouch asks Just paid? later.

Dynamic Island and quick logging

  • Safe today sits in the Dynamic Island.
  • One sheet for every category, with Income and Paid back.
  • Siri payments wait on Today under Add these?.

Dark mode

  • Today, a new entry and Budget in dark mode.
  • Every colour checked for contrast.
