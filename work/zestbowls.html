Overview

The idea

ZestBowls helps people find fresh, nutritious meals that fit their diet, from everyday bowls to calorie deficit plans.

Focus

Appetising visuals, simple navigation and clear nutrition details, so comparing meals and ordering takes seconds.

My role

I designed it end to end: the brand, the UI kit and every page.

Screens

Landing page, meals and cart

  • A bold promise, this week's deals and an easy path to order; calories, protein and ingredients on every meal; a cart with a clear total.

Look and feel

Colour, type and UI kit

  • A fresh green with Poppins, and buttons and cards built as one kit.
