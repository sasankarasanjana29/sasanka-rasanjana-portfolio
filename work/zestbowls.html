Overview

The idea

ZestBowls helps people find fresh, nutritious meals that fit their diet, from everyday bowls to calorie deficit plans.

Focus

Appetising visuals, simple navigation and clear nutrition details, so comparing meals and ordering takes seconds.

My role

I designed it end to end: the brand, the UI kit and every page.
