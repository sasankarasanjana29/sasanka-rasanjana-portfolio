Cloudia

A calm iOS app for the loose ends in your head: what you promised, and what someone owes you.

Overview

The problem

People keep small obligations in their heads, notes and screenshots: a refund due, a reply owed, money lent. They slip until they hurt.

The idea

Every item is a loop, either My turn (I need to do it) or Waiting (someone owes me). Both directions live in one calm place.

Principles

  • Never nags: reminders help, they never guilt.
  • Private by default: no ads, no tracking, no account needed.
  • Honest: it never claims a feature it does not have.

Screens

Today

  • A week strip and the few things that need you now, in light and dark.

Loops, both ways

  • My turn for what you owe, Waiting for what you are owed.
  • A late loop says how late, without shouting.

Add by hand or by voice

  • Say a sentence and Cloudia fills one card per fact: what, who, when, amount and category.
  • Speech stays on the device and nothing is recorded.

Staying in the clear

  • A streak of days in the clear: about staying on top of things, never about opening the app.

How I built it

Design

Figma components and a token system that the code shares, so light and dark stay in step.

Build

React Native with Expo and Reanimated for the motion, built with Claude Code.

Data

Local first: the phone is the source of truth, with an optional backup sync.
