Quick things about me

I loved art as a kid, and IT was always around me at home. My IT degree let me pick a path, so I chose UI/UX: the one place where my design side and software development meet.

I sketch a few screens of the basic idea first. Then I build the small parts, like buttons and inputs, try them in the app, and grow them into a design system before designing the full app. Now I hand that system to Claude Code and it carries the same look through the build.

The Keells redesign. It reached over 100,000 people and 2,000+ reactions on LinkedIn, and it helped me grow to about 3,500 followers there.

I welcome it. I listen, ask questions when something is unclear, and keep iterating until the design feels right.

Figma for UI and prototypes, and Claude Code to research, find ideas and build: React Native apps and websites I host on Lovable. ClickUp and Slack every day with my team.

Minimal, practical and realistic. Not too fancy: designs that solve the real problem and can actually be built.
