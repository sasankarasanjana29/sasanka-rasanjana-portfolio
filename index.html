Quick things about me

I have always loved making things. I started in graphic design as a portrait artist, and with my IT background, UX/UI let me bring creativity and tech together in a career I love.

Low-fidelity wireframes first, then the paths and interactions. I iterate on testing and feedback until the design is functional, clear and good to look at.

The Keells redesign. It was not a full case study, but it reached over 100,000 people and 2,000+ reactions on LinkedIn, and it connected me with the design community.

I welcome it. I listen carefully, ask questions when something is unclear, and iterate until the design feels right.

Figma for UI and prototypes, Framer for responsive sites, Jitter for animation, Illustrator and Photoshop for visuals, and Notion, ClickUp and Slack every day with my team. Lately also Claude Code and Lovable to build.

Simple, practical and realistic. Designs that look good, solve real problems and can actually be built without overcomplicating things.
